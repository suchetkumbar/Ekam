## Script / romanisation noise: how much does the normaliser undo?

On 3,000 sampled names, the normalised key is recovered exactly after Devanagari rendering in **78.6%** of cases and after romanisation variants in **97.0%**. The misses are held-out loanwords (e.g. लाइफ → LAIF) and re-spelled names that folding does not collapse; the matcher must handle them with fuzzy/phonetic similarity.
