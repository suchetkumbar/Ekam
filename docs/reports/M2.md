**Problems found**
* *Street line is a near-identifier (generator artefact).* 64% of hard-level true pairs keep
  an identical normalised street; street similarity ≥ 0.8 catches 85.5% of true pairs at a 1%
  false rate. LightGBM puts ~80% of its gain on street features. Real vendor records often
  use different addresses (works, branch, billing office).
* Identifiers barely matter after blocking (with vs without IDs: +0.002 F1 at hard).
* Splink EM blocked on PIN alone produced 1.5e8 pairs and exhausted memory → fixed (PIN + first
  token, phonetic + city); DuckDB spill now goes to `.cache/duckdb` with a 4 GB cap.
* Slow network: the CUDA torch wheel (2.75 GB) failed in uv; fetched with a resumable download,
  hash-checked against `uv.lock`, installed locally (uv sees the environment as in sync).
* Blocking, not matching, is the hard-level bottleneck: 11% of true pairs never reach the matcher.
